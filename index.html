How the push is applied

It adds one direction to the model's layer 23. 1× is the gap between an average stop turn and an average tool-call turn. On eight test tasks needing six tool calls, the agent made 5.8 with no push, 3.2 at 1× stop sooner, 0.5 at 2×, and 11.6 at 1× keep going.
